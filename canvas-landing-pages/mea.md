## About

This module provides hands-on experience in building a RESTful microservice with modern engineering practices. By working through this series of lessons, you'll gain the skills needed to develop, deploy, and maintain a cloud-connected microservice. You'll learn how to create an API, integrate it with a cloud database, and establish Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) templates to optimize future projects. This practice-oriented approach will help you build a solid foundation in microservice development.
